# 学术写作短语库 (Academic Phrase Bank)

本文件是 `sci-writestructure` skill 的**句式参考库**，与 `sci-writing-standard.md` 配套使用。
用途：在 Step 2 逐节审查、Step 3 产出「改写建议」时，按章节或功能检索贴合的英文句式骨架，替换变量后使建议可套用（作者可直接使用，但严禁原样照抄模板示例句）。

检索建议：用 Grep 按章节名（如 `A3`、`Discussion`、`gap`）或功能关键词（如 `However`、`hypothesized`、`consent`）在本文检索。

> **使用边界（重要）**：本库仅为**句式结构参考**，供借鉴骨架、替换变量之用。所有 `XXX`/`X`/`Y` 及省略号处必须由用户稿件的真实内容填充。库中少数带具体内容的示例句（如 `Because of the rarity of this cancer…`）仅为示意，**套用时须替换为用户实际研究对象**，严禁原样照抄填入稿件。详见 SKILL.md 质量准则「禁止逐字照搬」。

## A. 按章节句式模板（Section-by-section templates）

### A1. Title 标题
- `XXX attenuate XXX-induced XXX disease through/by the inhibition/regulation of XX`

### A2. Abstract 摘要

**结构框架**：一篇流畅段落，4 个功能组件的自然衔接。非分段式。

1) **开场（1 句）**：用事实性陈述点明研究背景与缺口。避免空洞的重要性声明。推荐采用"共识→But→争议/Gap"或"事实→yet→不确定性"的对比结构。
2) **方法/做法（1 句）**：固定句式 `Here we [动词] + [精确的研究范围、样本量、方法]`。本句只交代做了什么，不提前结论。
3) **发现（2-4 句，占篇幅 60% 以上）**：按逻辑次序逐条陈述核心发现。可以有转折、对比、量化数据。多发现之间使用 first/second/finally 或 however/whereas/in contrast 自然衔接。每个发现至少包含处理、效应方向、量级、比较基准之一。
4) **收尾推断（1 句）**：基于发现做具体推断。不要写 "further studies are needed"，也不要写 "These results highlight the importance of..."——要么写一个具体的科学推断，要么写研究对其他领域的启示。收尾句让读者带走一个结论，而非一个声明。

**格式惯例**：
- 数字首次出现用全称（"around 2,300 insect species"），后文可数字
- 单位与数字间空一格（0.5 °C min⁻¹，而非 0.5°C min⁻¹）
- 统计显著性用括号标注（p < 0.05），CI 用 `(95% CI = 下限, 上限)`
- 首次出现的缩写需写全称

**真实论文片段引用**（仅作节奏参考，非可复制句式）：
- 2026 Nature 昆虫热耐受：开场"Insects make up the majority of all animal species, with 70% occurring in the tropics, yet the impacts of warming on tropical insects remain highly uncertain."→ 方法"Here we compared... around 2,300 insect species along... elevational gradients and identified genomic signatures across the insect tree of life."→ 发现"We show that... do not proportionally track environmental temperatures but approach an asymptote in... Heat tolerance showed strong differences among insect orders and families, reflected in..."→ 收尾"Our data suggest a limited capacity of insects in the Earth's most biodiverse regions to buffer future warming."
- 2004 Nature 内蒙草原：开场"Numerous studies have suggested that... But this view has been challenged."→ 方法"On the basis of a 24-year study... here we present three key findings."→ 发现"First, that...; second, that...; and finally, that..."→ 收尾"Our study provides new insights for better management and restoration of the rapidly degrading Inner Mongolia grassland."
- 2015 Nature 草地多样性反弹：开场"The negative effect of... is now incontrovertible. However, the degree to which... can be expected to bounce back is uncertain."→ 方法"Here we present evidence from the 160-year-old Park Grass Experiment..."→ 发现"The proportion of legumes, species richness and diversity increased... Plots that stopped receiving N fertilizer recovered much of the diversity..."→ 收尾"There was no evidence that chronic N addition has resulted in an alternative low biodiversity state..."

### A3. Introduction 引言
1) **Background Known**（General → specific）。
2) **Unknown / gap**（如何填补、为何重要）：
   - `Until now, it has not been possible to identify ….`
   - `However, we do not know what effect this treatment will have on ….`
   - `There has been some disagreement, however, in the findings with this model….`
   - `So far, only qualitative findings have been obtained…`
   - `Because of the rarity of this cancer, it has been difficult to determine survival rates with statistical certainty…`
3) **Hypothesis**：
   - `The purpose of our study was to determine ….`
   - `We hypothesized that ….` / `Our hypothesis was that….`
4) **Strategy**：
   - `We tested our hypothesis by examining….`
   - `To do this, we used … and analyzed … in an in situ model ….`
   - `To evaluate/investigate/explore the inhibitory/regulatory effect of … on …., the … assay/methods/experiments were employed/used.`
5) **Conclusion**：
   - `Overall, our findings that … confirm/indicate/suggest/demonstrate ….`
   - `In this study / In the present study, we found/observed/demonstrated that ….`

### A4. Materials and Methods 材料与方法
1) **Materials and reagents**：Drugs, reagents, equipment, antibodies, etc.
2) **Cell lines and cultures**：Which cells used? Supplier? How prepared/cultured/treated/grouped? How cell-samples collected?
3) **Human subjects**：IRB approval; Informed consent; Source of study population; Inclusion/exclusion criteria; Sample collection and number.
4) **Animals**：Standards of animal care; Supplier; How many; How treated/grouped/killed; How samples collected (time points, volume, gram, surgery).
- **Experiment 1-4**：`To evaluate/examine/determine/detect/assess/measure …., we performed … assay/experiment.` + what was done and why.
- **Statistical tests/analysis**：comparisons/evaluation used; which tests; significance levels; software.

### A5. Results 结果

**段落结构（每段循环）**：主题句点明发现 → 数据支撑（数值+统计） → 交互/例外（如适用） → 图表引用。每段一个核心发现，段间以切换指标或因子过渡。

- **小标题建议**：`Effect of … on … in ….` / 或直接以指标命名，避免模糊。
- **主题句**：第一句直接点出该段核心结论——处理→指标→效应方向→量级。不绕弯、不铺垫。
- **数据支撑**：紧跟主题句之后。取决于分析类型，使用对应的统计格式（见下文「统计报告格式参照」）。
- **交互/例外**：多因子实验需报告主效应和交互效应（显著/不显著/边缘）。逐年分解时按时间顺序陈述。
- **图表引用**：放在数据支撑句尾的括号内：(Figure X, Table Y)。或异步引用（"as shown in Figure X"）。
- **段末小结**（非必须）：仅在需要总结复合发现时使用，避免每段都写 "Taken together"。

**统计报告格式参照**（以下为各方法的格式惯例，非填空模板，应根据具体数值填充）：

- **ANOVA / 线性模型**：报告 `F(效应df, 残差df) = 数值, P = 数值`
- **广义线性模型 / 逻辑回归**：报告 `χ²(df) = 数值, P = 数值`
- **效应量 + 置信区间**：报告 `效应量 (95% CI = 下限, 上限)` — CI 不包含 0 即为显著
- **回归系数**：报告 `β = 数值; 95% CI = 下限, 上限`，可附加 P 值
- **t 检验**：报告 `t(df) = 数值, P = 数值; 均值差 = 数值 (95% CI = 下限, 上限)`
- **生存分析**：报告 `HR = 数值, 95% CI = 下限, 上限, P = 数值`
- **组间比较（事后检验）**：以字母标记法或差值+CI 呈现，标记共享字母的处理间无显著差异
- **选择系数**：报告 `s = 数值`
- **系统发育信号**：报告 `Pagel's λ = 数值, P = 数值; Blomberg's K = 数值, P = 数值`
- **不显著结果**：明确注明 `P > 0.05` 或 CI 跨越零点，同时可备注趋势方向

**真实论文片段引用（仅作结构参考）**：
- 2026 Nature 昆虫热耐受："...the thermal tolerance range across the whole insect community did not significantly change with elevation (Neotropics: estimate = 0.00036, F₁,₂₃ = 1.785, P = 0.195... Afrotropics: estimate = 0.00003, F₁,₁₃ = 0.004, P = 0.951)."
- 2022 Nature Comms 昆虫热可塑性："For every 1 °C rise in acclimation temperature, CTmax increased by 0.091 °C (95% CI = 0.030, 0.153)."
  - 同一论文中不显著的报告方式："we found no relationship between latitude and ARR (CTmax βARR = −0.001; 95% CI = −0.002, 0.001)."
- 2025 ES&T 农药耐受："Chlorpyrifos decreased survival in sensitive clones (−10.1%, P = 0.023 at low; −30.5%, P < 0.001 at high), while it did not affect tolerant clones (both concentrations: P > 0.60) (Figure 2)."
- 你的原文（氮添加研究）："Both year and N addition significantly affected species richness, however, no significant interactions were detected (Table 1)."

### A6. Discussion 讨论（段落顺序）
1) **Conclusion**（基于主要发现、回扣目的/假设，首段）：
   - `Our findings that … confirm ….` / `In this study, we found that …` / `Our results indicate/suggest/show that ….`
2) **Interpretation**：进一步解释，帮助读者理解研究重要性。
3) **Fit with literature**：
   - 一致：`Our data are consistent with ….` / `Our findings on … agree with those reported by … et al., who …..`
   - 相悖：`Our data differ from …` / `Unlike … et al., we observed that ….`
4) **Novelty / strength**（可选）：`In this study, we showed for the first time that ….` / `The major strength of this study was …`
5) **Limitation**：`Our study subjects were …, so it is not known whether our results are applicable to groups….` / `Our study had several limitations. First …..`
6) **Generalizations**：`Although our cohort was limited to …, the results suggest that …`
7) **Why gap matters**：`Our findings will allow us to take the next step in ….`
8) **Implications / speculation**：`Our findings may be useful in ….` / `We speculate that …` / `Our results imply that … but this must be tested further ….` / `These findings are important for … and point to the need for …`
9) **Avenues for further study**：`The next step is to …` / `Additional studies are needed to confirm …` / `Larger studies with longer follow-up are needed to …`

### A7. Acknowledgment 致谢与声明
- `This study was supported by XXX; we thank Dr. XXX for XXX support/help.`
- `Disclosures: The authors have no financial conflicts of interest.`

### A8. Tables and Figures 图表
- Tables: Table 1, Table 2 …；Figures: Figure 1, Figure 2, Figure 3 …。
- Figure legend：`Figure 1: …..`

## B. 按功能通用短语库（Functional phrase bank）

### B1. 描述观点 / 领域重要性 —— 适用：Abstract（引言背景）, Introduction
1. It is becoming increasingly difficult to ignore the .....
2. X is the leading cause of death in western industrialised countries.
3. The issue of X has received considerable critical attention.
4. Central to the entire discipline of X is the concept of .......
5. X is at the heart of our understanding of ......

### B2. 强调研究领域的重要问题 —— 适用：Introduction
1. However, a major problem with this kind of application is ......
2. Lack of X has existed as a health problem for many years.
3. Despite its safety and efficacy, X suffers from several major drawbacks:
4. However, research has consistently shown that first-year students have not attained an adequate understanding of ...... There is increasing concern that some Xs are being disadvantaged ......
5. Questions have been raised about the safety of prolonged use of ......

### B3. 强调研究领域存在的争议 —— 适用：Introduction, Discussion
1. To date there has been little agreement on what ......

3. In many Xs a debate is taking place between Ys and Zs concerning ......
4. Debate continues about the best strategies for the management of ......

### B4. 强调知识差距（gap）—— 适用：Introduction

4. No previous study has investigated X.

### B5. 强调前人研究的不足之处 —— 适用：Introduction

- 样品特性：

- 陈述结果：

  5. The most interesting finding was that ......

- 重申研究目的：

  5. Both qualitative and quantitative methods were used in this investigation.

- 表明局限性：

- 研究框架：

  9. The final chapter draws upon the entire thesis, tying up the various theoretical and empirical strands in order to ......
- 解释关键术语：

### B12. 文章重点：理论 / 方法层面的问题与局限 —— 适用：Discussion

## C. 按章节补充句式（中英对照）

从原句库精选被删条目中结构独特的高质量句式，按文章章节重新归组织，保留中英对照便于快速理解使用场景。

### C1. Introduction 引言

- `However, the evidence for this relationship is inconclusive ……` 然而，此关系的证据尚无定论……
- `This indicates a need to understand the various perceptions of … that exist among …` 这表明需要了解……之间存在的各种……认知
- `The existing accounts fail to resolve the contradiction between X and Y.` 现有的论述未能解决 X 与 Y 之间的矛盾
- `X is an important component in Y, and plays a key role in Z.` X 是 Y 的重要组成部分，并在 Z 中发挥关键作用

### C2. Methods 方法

- `A random sample of patients with … was recruited from …` 从……招募了……患者的随机样本
- `Eligible … who matched the selection criteria were identified by …` 符合纳入标准的……通过……被确定
- `… are the main non-invasive method used to determine …` ……是用于确定……的主要非侵入性方法

### C3. Results 结果

- `X provided the largest set of significant clusters of …` X 提供了……的最大显著聚类集
- `This experiment did not detect any evidence for ……` 该实验未检测到……的任何证据

### C4. Discussion 讨论

- `In reviewing the literature, no data was found on the association between X and Y.` 在文献回顾中，未发现关于 X 与 Y 之间关联的数据
- `The main limitation of this method, however, is …` 然而，该方法的主要限制是……
- `All the studies reviewed so far, however, suffer from the fact that ……` 然而，迄今为止所有被综述的研究都存在……的问题
- `This study set out with the aim of assessing the importance of X in ……` 本研究旨在评估 X 在……中的重要性

### C5. Conclusion 结论

- `This study has argued that X is the best instrument to ……` 本研究论证了 X 是……的最佳工具
- `This project was undertaken to design …… and evaluate …` 本项目的目的是设计……并评估……
